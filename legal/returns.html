Returns Policy

At Next Laptops UG, we want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not satisfied, we are here to help.

1. Return Period

You may return any laptop purchased from Next Laptops UG within 7 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange. After this period, returns may be handled under our warranty policy (if applicable).

2. Condition for Returns

To be eligible for a return, your item must be:

  • In the same condition that you received it.
  • Unused and with all original tags attached.
  • In the original packaging with all accessories included.

Any signs of damage, misuse, or unauthorized repairs may void the return policy.

3. Return Process

To initiate a return:

  • Contact our customer service team at nextlaptops256@gmail.com or call +256 771 368 136.
  • Provide your order details and reason for return.
  • Our team will guide you through the return process and arrange for pickup if necessary.

4. Refunds

Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

  • Approved Refunds: Will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original method of payment.
  • Partial Refunds: May be granted for items returned with missing parts or signs of usage.

5. Warranty Claims

If your laptop develops a fault within the warranty period, please refer to the warranty card included with your purchase. Warranty terms differ from standard returns and usually involve repair or replacement.

6. Exceptions

Customized or special order laptops may not be eligible for return unless they are defective. Please check with our sales team before making a purchase if you have specific concerns.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about our returns policy, please don't hesitate to contact us. We are here to ensure you have a smooth experience.
